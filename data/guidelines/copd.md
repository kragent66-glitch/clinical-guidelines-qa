# GOLD Global Strategy for COPD Diagnosis and Management (2026)

## SECTION 1: Diagnosis and Severity Classification
Diagnosing COPD and classifying airflow obstruction.

*   **Diagnosis**: Spirometry showing post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC < 0.70 confirms persistent airflow limitation.
*   **GOLD Airflow Obstruction Classification (Based on FEV1 % predicted)**:
    *   **GOLD 1 (Mild)**: FEV1 >= 80% predicted.
    *   **GOLD 2 (Moderate)**: 50% <= FEV1 < 80% predicted.
    *   **GOLD 3 (Severe)**: 30% <= FEV1 < 50% predicted.
    *   **GOLD 4 (Very Severe)**: FEV1 < 30% predicted.

---

## SECTION 2: Pharmacological Treatment Pathway (GOLD ABE Schema)
Initial maintenance therapy groups (A, B, and E) based on symptoms and exacerbation history.

*   **Group A (0-1 moderate exacerbations, no hospitalizations, CAT score < 10)**: Single bronchodilator (SABA, LAMA, or LABA).
*   **Group B (0-1 moderate exacerbations, no hospitalizations, CAT score >= 10)**: LABA + LAMA combination (e.g., Indacaterol/Glycopyrronium 110/50 mcg daily).
*   **Group E (>= 2 moderate exacerbations or >= 1 leading to hospitalization)**:
    *   **Preferred**: LAMA + LABA combination.
    *   **If Blood Eosinophils >= 300 cells/uL**: Triple therapy (LABA + LAMA + ICS) (e.g., Fluticasone furoate/Umeclidinium/Vilanterol 100/62.5/25 mcg daily).

---

## SECTION 3: Clinical Warnings and Safety
*   **Pneumonia Risk**: Inhaled corticosteroids (ICS) increase the risk of pneumonia in COPD patients. Only use ICS when blood eosinophils are high (>= 300 cells/uL) or there is a history of asthma overlap.
*   **Oxygen Therapy Warning**: Long-term oxygen therapy (>15 hours/day) is indicated ONLY for severe resting hypoxemia (PaO2 <= 55 mmHg or SaO2 <= 88%). Targeting SaO2 88-92% is preferred to avoid suppressing respiratory drive in hypercapnic patients.
*   **Cardiovascular Comorbidities**: COPD patients are at high risk for ischemic heart disease and arrhythmias. Beta-blockers are NOT contraindicated in COPD and should be used if clinically indicated (cardioselective preferred, e.g., Metoprolol).
